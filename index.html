What to confirm in writing

  • Commission rate and what it applies to
  • Marketplace, payment processing, and shipping fees
  • Insurance and responsibility for items in transit
  • Minimum accepted value and pricing authority
  • Returns, cancellations, chargebacks, and disputes
  • Payout method, timing, and unsold-item return policy

Do not send cards until both parties have agreed to the final terms and shipping instructions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept every collection?

No. Each submission should be reviewed for fit, value, condition, and available capacity. Acceptance must be confirmed in writing.

Is the 25% commission final?

No. Commission can be subject to change depending on value of the card/ or collection.

When do sellers get paid?

Sellers should expect payment upon final sale of their card/collection.

Can I submit photos?

Yes. Add a shared photo link to the form or attach photos to the email it prepares. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

What happens if my cards do not sell?

Your agreement should define the listing period, price adjustments, relisting, and who pays for return shipping.
